One tap turns on your Out of Office everywhere

OutSwitch is a mobile app that connects to your workplace communication tools and lets you activate or deactivate your Out of Office status across all of them at once — with a single tap.

How it works

1
Connect your accounts — Slack, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, and WhatsApp — once during setup.
2
When you're heading out, tap the OutSwitch button. Your Out of Office status is set everywhere instantly.
3
Set a return date and OutSwitch automatically turns everything off when you're back — no action needed.
